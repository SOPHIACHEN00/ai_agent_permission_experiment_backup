Both figures are actual dependency-free SVGs generated by ../summarize_evidence.py from the verified tables. They have zero-based axes, no smoothing, no significance markers, and show every seed instead of error bars. SVG/XML parsing passed. They are evidence plots, not a claim of publication-layout or visual-render QA.

## figure-01-paired-seeds.svg

- Purpose: reveal whether mean improvement is shared across seeds.
- Data: ../postflight_verification.json and ../per_seed_results.csv; four configurations×three paired seeds, each value averages four fixed validation cells.
- Caption: colored/shaped points identify seeds101/211/307; dashed connecting segments indicate pairing across categorical configurations, not time or interpolation. Black diamonds are three-seed means. Horizontal dashed line is the common mean DP optimum.7075. No CI/error bars; n=3.
- Notice: seed307 rises from zero, seed101 improves, seed211 decreases for all three modifications. B has the largest observed mean.
- Implication: retain the full distribution; a larger mean does not imply all-seed or population improvement. Confirm on new seeds before stronger claims.

## figure-02-best-config-cells.svg

- Purpose: identify remaining deficits within selected configuration B.
- Data: ../exact_values.csv, B only; all three seeds at br05/br06×original/prompt_toggle. Points are slightly horizontally offset only for visibility.
- Caption: y-axis exact success probability; x-axis four fixed validation cells; three seed points per cell. Short black dashed segments give each cell's DP optimum. No aggregation error bars, smoothing, or significance markers.
- Notice: seed307 reaches the two original-condition optima but remains below both prompt_toggle optima, especially br06 (.52 versus.80). Other seeds also have losses.
- Implication: inspect existing prompt_toggle decisions before more broad parameter search. This plot does not identify the unique mechanism, and B was selected on these validation data.
